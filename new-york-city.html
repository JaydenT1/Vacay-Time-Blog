About

New York City (NYC), the most populous city in the US, is best known as the home to Statue of Liberty, World Trade Center, and Empire State Building.

Time

New York is in Eastern Time Zone (ET).
Daylight time (EDT) is 4 hours behind UTC, and standard time (EST) is 5 hours behind UTC.

Clock provided by timeanddate.com

Weather

I went to NYC in late November, and it was quite cold.
It wasn't at freezing temperature yet, but it was consistently below 10°C (50°F).

In the following December, the temperature was consistently freezing.

Transportation

Various of public transporation are available in New York City.


For travelling within NYC:
Transit Base Fares (As of April 2026) Description
MTA Subway $3/1-way Good for avoiding the traffic.
MTA Bus $3/1-way; $7.25/1-way for expresses buses; Q70 is free. Higher coverage than subway.
NYC Ferry $4.5/1-way A different perspective to look at NYC.
TLC Taxi $3 initial charge; 0.7/0.2mi or /60s during slow traffic; plus surcharge and extra fees on situations such as overnight, rush hour, congestion zone, and toll Costly but convenient.
For travelling between nearby cities and NYC:
MTA LIRR/Metro-North Varies. Based on when and where you're travelling. Can reach Long Island/North of NYC/New Haven.
PATH $3/1-way Can reach Jersey City/Hoboken/Newark.
NJ Transit Commuter Rail Varies. Based on zones. Can reach Trenton/Philadelphia.
NJ Transit Bus Varies. Based on zones. High coverage of New Jersey.

1 free transfer within 2 hours of paying. (subway to bus/bus to subway/bus to bus)
For subway to subway, please refer to the MTA subway system map. (For most stations, a free transfer is only available if the stations are connected underground)

MTA subway and bus are the best options for travelling in NYC. Utilize the free transfer system to save the most.

There is also a fare cap at $35/week on subway and local bus, or $67/week including express bus.
